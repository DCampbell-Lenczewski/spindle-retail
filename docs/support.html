Common questions

What's the difference between gap and centres?

The gap is the clear space between two spindles. Centres is the distance from the middle of one spindle to the middle of the next, so it's the gap plus one spindle thickness. Centres is usually what you mark out on the string or base rail.

What does the 100mm check mean?

UK Building Regulations say a 100mm sphere must not pass through any gap in the guarding on stairs and landings in homes. Spindle Mate keeps the gap at 99mm or less. If a result fails, tap Tap to fix and it works out the fewest spindles that pass.

How do turned spindles work?

Switch on Turned. The thickness you enter is the square plinth block at each end of the spindle. The app gives you the plinth gap, which is the gap between the square blocks. If you enter the neck size, it also checks the gap at the thinnest part of the spindle.

How do I measure the stair angle?

Open the angle measurement and lay the long edge of your phone along the handrail, string or nosings, then read the angle. If readings look off, calibrate by laying the phone on a surface you have checked with a spirit level. For the best result, take the phone out of a bulky case.

The price in the app is different from the website.

Retailers change their prices and products often. The price on the retailer's website is always the one that applies. Let us know and we'll update the list.

The shop finder shows nothing near me.

The shop finder opens Apple Maps. Check that Location Services is on for Maps in your iPhone's Settings.
