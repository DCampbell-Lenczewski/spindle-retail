Privacy policy

Last updated 23 November 2026

In short: Spindle Mate does not collect, store or sell any personal information. There are no accounts, adverts, analytics or tracking in the app.

What stays on your phone

Your measurements, settings and last-used values are saved only on your device so they're there next time you open the app. They are never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them.

Motion sensors

The pitch-measuring feature reads your phone's motion sensors to work out the stair angle. The readings are used on your phone at that moment and are not stored or sent anywhere.

Location

Spindle Mate does not access your location. When you use the shop finder, the app opens Apple Maps with a search, for example "builders merchant". Apple Maps may use your location under Apple's privacy policy.

Internet connections the app makes

  • Product list: the app downloads the current list of products and prices from GitHub. GitHub receives standard technical information such as your IP address, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.
  • App updates: small fixes are delivered through Expo's update service. It receives standard technical information such as your IP address, app version and device platform, as described in Expo's privacy policy.
  • Product links: before opening a product, the app checks that the retailer's page still exists. If it has gone, the app opens the retailer's search instead.

None of these connections includes your name, contact details or measurements.

Shop links and commission

Some "Buy online" links are affiliate links, run by Awin and the retailers. When you tap one, your browser opens the retailer's website. Awin and the retailer may use cookies so that we get a commission if you buy something. This costs you nothing extra. What happens on their websites is covered by their own privacy policies, including Awin's privacy policy. We only receive anonymous sales totals from Awin, never who bought what.

Children

Spindle Mate is a trade tool and is not aimed at children. It doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Your rights

Because we don't hold personal information about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have any questions about this policy, contact us using the details on the support page.

Changes

If the app ever starts collecting information, this page will be updated before that change is released. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
